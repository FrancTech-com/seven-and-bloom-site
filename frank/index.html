About Francis

I’m a Makerere University student pursuing Information Systems and Technology. Am from a small town in West nile region called Parombo in Nebbi district.

Outside of academics, I run Etheles Studio which is a start up digital agency, where I focus on building digital products and systems that are both functional and user-friendly. I enjoy exploring new technologies, learning from real-world projects, and sharing my experiences with the community.

When I’m not coding or designing, you can find me reading about tech trends, experimenting with new tools, or engaging in campus activities. I believe in continuous learning and applying knowledge to create meaningful solutions.
