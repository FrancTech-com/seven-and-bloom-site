Featured business

Tropical Twist Juice

Freshness with a twist ♡

Tropical Twist Juice is my small business where I turn fresh fruits into refreshing, natural juices. We serve flavour, energy and good vibes at market days, functions, parties and events. It’s more than juice — it’s a lifestyle!

Book us! ♡

Tropical Twist is an idea that is growing into something real, one event at a time. Every glass starts with real fruit, blended into natural juices — Pineapple Mint, Mango Coconut, Passion Fruit and Watermelon Beetroot are some of our favourites.

I love the setting just as much as the juice: glass dispensers, mason jars with straws, fresh flowers and a styled table that makes the juice stand part of the celebration. Whether it’s a wedding, a graduation, a baby shower or a busy market day, we bring colour, freshness and good energy.

Running a business while studying has taught me to plan, to handle challenges and to keep showing up. I share those lessons here as the journey continues.

About Me ♡

Hey, I’m Gloria — a girl with big dreams and a heart full of possibilities.

I’m a Makerere University student pursuing Information Systems and Technology, a young entrepreneur, a food lover, and a creative soul who believes that life is about more than simply existing — it’s about learning, growing, creating, and becoming.

I’m passionate about technology and its power to solve real-world problems, but I’m also drawn to the little things that make life beautiful: good food, meaningful relationships, faith, personal growth, and moments that turn into memories.

Through my entrepreneurial journey, I’m learning to turn ideas into something real, embrace challenges, and build something meaningful from the ground up. Through university, I’m discovering new possibilities, developing my skills, and learning more about the person I want to become.

My journey isn’t about having everything figured out. It’s about showing up, learning from my experiences, celebrating the little wins, and trusting God with the bigger picture.

Gloria’s Corner is my little space to document it all — the lessons, the dreams, the business ideas, the adventures, the faith, the food, and the beautiful, sometimes messy process of growing into myself.

I’m still becoming, still dreaming, and still writing my story. And there’s so much more to come.

One girl. Many dreams. A life still unfolding. ♡
